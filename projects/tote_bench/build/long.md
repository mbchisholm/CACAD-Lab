## Assemble

Drill a pilot hole for every screw within 2 in of a board's end, so the pine does not split.

**1. Feet.** In the bottom end of every leg (10 legs), centred, drill for the tee nut and tap it in.
Use the hole size on the tee nut's packet. Thread a leveling foot in about halfway.

**2. Ladders (5, all identical).** Each is one front leg, one back leg and four rails.

- Lay a front leg (33-1/4 in) and a back leg (27-1/4 in) on the floor on edge, a 1-1/2 in face up, parallel, with their tops lined up against a straight edge.
- Lay a top rail across them, on edge. Its top edge is flush with the leg tops, and its ends are flush with the legs' outside edges, so the legs are 31-1/2 in apart outside to outside.
- On both legs, mark 17-3/4 in down from the top. Lay a mid rail with its top edge on the marks, ends flush the same way.
- Screw each rail to each leg with 2 screws through the rail: at 1-1/2 in and 2-3/4 in below the rail's top edge, centred on the leg's 1-1/2 in face. The screw runs into the leg's 3-1/2 in width. 8 screws per side.
- Check the ladder is square (equal diagonals), flip it, and fasten the other two rails to the other face the same way.
- 16 screws per ladder, 80 in all. The back leg ends 6 in short of the front leg: that is the lip.

**3. Stand the ladders** against the wall, front legs on the floor and back legs on the lip, the back legs 1/4 in off the wall.

**4. Shelf slats (spacers too).** In each tote column, lay 5 slats flat across the two mid rails. The slat ends butt against the leg faces on both sides, so the slats set the column width (20-5/8 in between rail faces).
- Back slat's back edge flush with the back legs' back faces, front slat's front edge flush with the front legs' front faces, the rest evenly spaced (1 in gaps).
- 2 screws down through the slat into each rail it crosses, centred on the rail: 60 in all.

**5. Footrest.** Set the knee-space ladders 23-5/8 in apart between rail faces. Screw the 33-5/8 in footrest on edge across the front faces of the two front legs. Its ends are flush with the legs' far edges. Its top edge is 8-3/4 in above the leg bottoms, 10 in off the floor with the feet at mid-travel.
- 2 screws into each leg at 1-1/2 in and 2-3/4 in below its top edge: 4 in all.

**6. Top.** Lay the 6 planks across all the top rails. The back plank is 1/4 in off the wall, and the planks are tight to each other. They overhang the end rails by 1 in and the front legs by 1-1/2 in.
- 2 screws down through each plank into every top rail it crosses, centred on the rail: 120 in all. Check that the bench is square before the last ones go in.

**7. Upper tote stops.** In each tote column, lay a stop (2x4, flat) on the back slat, back edges flush. 4 screws down into the slat: 12 in all.

**8. Level.** Turn the feet until the top is level both ways. Each foot moves about 1/2 in either way from the start, enough for the slab and the lip.

**9. Totes.** Slide each lower tote in, handle out, until it touches the lip. Slide each upper tote in until it touches the stop.

Optional: once you know where the wall studs are, drive a couple of long screws through the back of the top rails into them. The bench can't go backward against the lip, and this keeps it from racking along the wall.

## Check

Tote lid to slat underside 1-1/16 in with the slab 1/2 in high under it; upper lid to planks 1-1/16 in. Screws: 276, every one checked in the model to pass 1-1/2 in through its board and bite 1 in into the next, and to meet no other screw.
